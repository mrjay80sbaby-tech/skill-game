Privacy Policy — Skill Arcade

Last updated: November 14, 2026. Skill Arcade is a free skill game by MC Design & Freelancing (Justin Adam Kidd). Contact: visionary.volume.2@gmail.com.

What we collect

Skill Arcade does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, or any personal information, and does not sell or share personal data with anyone.

Local storage

Your coin balance, daily bonus status, and personal bests are stored only in your own browser (local storage) on your device. Clearing your browser data resets them. We do not receive or store this information on any server.

Advertising

On game portals such as CrazyGames, advertisements are served by the portal through its own advertising SDK. The portal and its advertising partners may use cookies or device identifiers to serve and measure ads, under the portal's own privacy policy. Skill Arcade itself does not run its own ad network.

Payments

The portal version of Skill Arcade has no in-game purchases and no payment flow. The separate version on our own website offers optional coin packs through Stripe checkout; payments there are processed by Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy, and we do not see or store your card details.

Children

Skill Arcade is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here. Questions: visionary.volume.2@gmail.com.
